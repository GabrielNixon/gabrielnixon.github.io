### Q1 — Inference under wrong models
Stress-adaptive updates (entropy-regularized trust decay) and **belief bandwidth** to modulate confidence when structure drifts.  
Fenchel-dual link: exponential tilting ↔ mirror descent with KL constraints.  
Current work defines a **fragility index** and shows sublinear dynamic-regret under regime shifts.

### Q2 — Fragile coordination
Coordination failures from misaligned incentives and partial views.  
Case studies: medical supply chains, HPC telemetry, and **ontology alignment** as optimization over uncertain equivalences.
