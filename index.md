### Research Focus

- **Q1: Inference under wrong models**  
  Entropy-regularized trust decay, belief bandwidth, and a fragility index for sequential decision-making under drift.

- **Q2: Fragile coordination**  
  Incentives and partial information causing global failures from local rationality; applications to supply chains, HPC telemetry, and ontology alignment.
